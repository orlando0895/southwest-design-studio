The Studio

Designed in Scottsdale, for Scottsdale.

Southwest Design Studio is the interior design showroom of Southwest Corridor, the Scottsdale group behind some of the Valley's most trusted home building and contracting companies. The studio brings the group's design-build know-how into a space anyone can walk into.

Why a Showroom

Because choosing finishes from a screen is a gamble.

Paint looks different at 4 PM than it does at 10 AM. Boucle feels different than it photographs. The studio exists so you can make every decision with your own eyes and hands, surrounded by designers who do this every day.

It is also where our trade partners, builders, and architects bring their clients: one address on Hayden Road for the region's design community.

The Group

One roof, nine crafts.

Southwest Corridor unites design, architecture, engineering, construction, mechanical, solar, pools, and smart home technology under one Scottsdale roof. The Design Studio is where those crafts meet the finishing layer: the rooms people actually live in.
