The Studio

Designed in Scottsdale, for Scottsdale.

Southwest Design Studio is the interior design showroom of Southwest Corridor, the Scottsdale group behind some of the Valley's most trusted home building and contracting companies. The studio brings the group's design-build know-how into a two-floor space anyone can walk into.

Because choosing finishes from a screen is a gamble.

Stone looks different at 4 PM than it does at 10 AM. A cabinet door feels different than it photographs. A fireplace is a different size in a room than on a spec sheet. The studio exists so you can make every decision with your own eyes and hands, surrounded by designers who do this every day.

It is also where our trade partners, builders, and architects bring their clients: one address on Hayden Road for the region's design community.

A showroom is the only honest way to choose a finish. You see it in real light, at real scale, next to everything else.

One roof, every craft.

Southwest Corridor unites design, architecture, engineering, construction, mechanical, solar, pools, and smart home technology under one Scottsdale roof. The Design Studio is where those crafts meet the finishing layer: the rooms people actually live in.
