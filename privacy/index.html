Privacy policy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

When you request a private preview or send us your visit list, we collect what you type into the form: your name, phone number, email address, project notes and the rooms or finishes you saved. Your saved visit list is stored only in your own browser until you send it.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about your visit or project and to have your finishes ready when you arrive. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not send it to anyone outside Southwest Design Studio and the Southwest Corridor companies working on your project.

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to us by a third-party email service. Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, to run and secure the site. Map directions open in Google Maps, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, call (877) 646-0167 or visit us at 15001 N Hayden Rd Ste 111, Scottsdale, AZ 85260, and we will take care of it.
